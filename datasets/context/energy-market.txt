Electricity systems continuously balance generation and demand. System operators
schedule generators, monitor frequency, procure operating reserves, and use
transmission networks to move power between regions. Variable renewable
generation changes with weather, while storage can absorb surplus electricity
and discharge it later. High-voltage transmission connects generation resources
to distribution networks, and distribution systems deliver lower-voltage power
to consumers. Market prices can signal scarcity, congestion, fuel costs, and the
value of flexibility. Reliable operation requires enough capacity, energy,
reserves, and network capability to respond to expected conditions and
unexpected outages.
